What the flow estimate means
Smallest t ≥ 0 such that
Σ max(cᵢ − hᵢ − tμᵢ, 0) ≤ Σ max(hᵢ + tμᵢ − cᵢ, 0) / rateᵢ

Income and trades are treated as fractional averages. Mean production hides droughts and correlated rolls, so two positions with equal pips can wait very differently. A zero estimate does not prove whole-card affordability; the Now column does. Ignores player trades, future robber moves, discards and bank shortages.

Investigate

Does a city or settlement pay back?

Extra cards compared with building nothing. A city adds one production unit, not two. Treats all cards as equal value. Ignores the robber, shortages, reinvestment, VP and expansion value.

How much does my surplus buy?

Uses your current hand and whole-card trades. A 2:1 port doubles long-run conversion versus 4:1; small hands round down. A willing player's 1:1 trade can beat any port.

Is this port worth building toward?
Value ≈ extra useful imports − cost of reaching the port − production given up

Sheep is not automatically the best port. Judge surplus against your plan: development cards spend sheep; cities spend ore and wheat. Late in the game, wood or brick often becomes the surplus.
