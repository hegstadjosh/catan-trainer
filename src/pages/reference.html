Useful deductions

Resource balance

  • Wheat − sheep = 2 × city upgrades. Sheep's direct demand is always ≤ wheat's for paid recipes.
  • Wood = brick in paid construction. Unequal production creates a bottleneck or a trading job.

Payback

  • City payback = 180 / site pips table rolls in an equal-card, no-robber model. At 12 pips: 15 rolls.
  • Settlement payback = 36(4 + 2r) / pips. Charge it the roads needed to reach it.
  • One round ≈ four table rolls in four-player play. You collect on other players' turns too.

Risk

  • Holding >7 on a 7 loses ⌊hand/2⌋ cards. A purchase can reduce exposure, but do not count those savings as guaranteed.
  • Same-number tiles are correlated. The robber blocks one hex, not every hex with that number.
